challenge_text: Hudson Designs was created as a concept for freelance designers looking for a simple, elegant way to showcase their work online. The goal was to demonstrate how a clean, minimal website can put a designer's portfolio front and centre without unnecessary distractions. The project focuses on thoughtful typography, subtle animation and carefully balanced layouts to create a professional first impression.

approach_text: Rather than relying on heavy visuals or complicated interactions, the focus was on creating a website that feels refined, confident and effortless to navigate. Every page was hand-coded from scratch, with careful attention paid to spacing, typography and smooth interactions that complement the designers content instead of trying to compete with it.
approach_tick_1: A minimalist portfolio designed to let creative work take centre stage.
approach_tick_2: Interactive project galleries that expand to showcase selected work.
approach_tick_3: A downloadable CV for potential clients and employers.
approach_tick_4: A testimonial section to reinforce trust and professional credibility.

testimonial_text: The website feels clean, modern and incredibly easy to navigate. It showcases the work without ever getting in the way.
testimonial_span: Concept Project

built_text: A refined portfolio website designed to present creative work in a confident and professional way. The layout balances generous whitespace, elegant typography and subtle animation to create an experience that feels polished without becoming distracting. Visitors can browse featured projects, learn about the designer's experience, download a CV and explore testimonials, all within a simple and intuitive interface.

result_text: Hudson Designs demonstrates how a portfolio can become part of a designer's personal brand rather than simply a collection of projects. Every section is designed to reinforce professionalism while keeping the focus firmly on the work itself.
result_tick_1: A clean, editorial-inspired design that gives creative work room to stand out.
result_tick_2: Interactive project galleries that encourage visitors to explore featured work in more detail.
result_tick_3: A clear professional profile combining experience, testimonials and downloadable CV in one place.
result_tick_4: Subtle animations and thoughtful interactions that create a polished, premium browsing experience.
