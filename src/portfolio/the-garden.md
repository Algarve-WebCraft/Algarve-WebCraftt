challenge_text: The Garden had built a fantastic reputation over the past 14 years without ever having a website. Most customers discovered the restaurant through word of mouth or just by walking past. While that worked well, there was no central place for visitors to view the menu, browse photos of the restaurant or learn its story before visiting. The owner wanted their first website to reflect the atmosphere they had spent years creating, while making it easier for new customers to discover everything the restaurant has to offer.

approach_text: Before writing a single line of code, I spent time understanding what made The Garden different. Rather than focusing on online bookings or complicated features, the goal was to create a website that captured the restaurant's personality and history. The site was designed and hand-coded from scratch with a strong focus on being different and maybe even a little weird.
approach_tick_1: A fully responsive design that works on every device.
approach_tick_2: Digital food and drinks menus that are easy to keep up to date.
approach_tick_3: A large photo gallery showcasing the restaurant, its history and its team.
approach_tick_4: Multiple language support for both locals and visitors.

testimonial_text: Working directly with one developer made everything so much easier. Changes were handled quickly and we always knew who to speak to.
testimonial_span: Kevin Lortan · Owner, The Garden Bar & Grill

built_text: The finished website reflects the relaxed atmosphere of The Garden while giving customers all the information they need before visiting. Fast loading pages, simple navigation and a high-quality gallery help showcase the restaurant without getting in the way. The website is fully managed, including hosting, updates and ongoing support, allowing the owners to focus entirely on running the restaurant.

result_text: The Garden now has a professional online presence that matches the quality of the restaurant itself. Customers can easily browse the menu, explore hundreds of photos and find everything they need before visiting. The website also provides a solid foundation for future updates as the business continues to devlop over the years.
result_tick_1: Easy-to-browse menus that customers can access on any device.
result_tick_2: An extensive photo gallery highlighting the restaurant, its story and the people behind it.
result_tick_3: Google maps and contact information to make planning a visit effortless.
result_tick_4: A fast, modern website that gives The Garden an online presence to match its reputation.
