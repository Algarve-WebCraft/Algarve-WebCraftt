faq_answer_5: "No. I believe in being completely transparent about pricing, so you will never be surprised by any hidden fees or unexpected charges. faq_answer_6: "It depends on the plan you choose. With the one-time build, you own the website and all of its source code and free to do with it as you please. This allows me to provide ongoing hosting, maintenance, updates, and support as part of the service while keeping the monthly cost affordable."

faq_answer_7: "That depends on which plan you're on. If you chose the one-time build, you are free to cancel any ongoing support or maintenance services whenever you like. faq_question_8: "Can i cancel after the six months and still keep the website?"
faq_answer_8: "No. With the monthly plan, the website remains part of the ongoing service, so if you cancel your subscription, the website and its source code are not transferred to you. <br><br> The monthly plan is designed to provide an affordable way to have a professional website without the large upfront cost of a one-time build.
